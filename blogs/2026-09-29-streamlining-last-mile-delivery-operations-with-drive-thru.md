For last-mile delivery providers and distribution hubs, fleet uptime directly impacts daily package throughput and customer satisfaction. High daily mileage, frequent stops, and tight delivery schedules put continuous stress on delivery vans and box trucks. Traditional fleet management relies heavily on manual driver walkarounds and Driver Vehicle Inspection Reports (DVIRs).
