For commercial fleet operators and logistics providers, unscheduled downtime is one of the single largest threats to operational profitability. Managing tight schedules, middle-mile transit legs, and driver changeovers makes routine safety compliance a constant challenge. Additionally, traditional inspection methods rely heavily on driver walkarounds and manual Driver Vehicle Inspection Reports (DVIRs).
