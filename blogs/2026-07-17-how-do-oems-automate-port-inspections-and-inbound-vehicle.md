Managing vehicle logistics gateways at high-volume ports has traditionally relied on slow, manual inspections. For Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), vehicles moving across ship, rail, or truck transit legs are highly exposed to transit-induced defects. Clipboard checks leave OEMs vulnerable to costly damage disputes and repair rework.
